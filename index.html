02 / Current study

Rocket
structures

I’m designing a fiberglass airframe with Cornell Rocketry and modeling its structural loads in Ansys.

I’m modeling axial compression, pure bending, and torsion for six- and eight-ply fiberglass layups, using the results to inform airframe thickness and layup decisions.

Analysis in progress
01

Model the airframe

Build the airframe geometry in SolidWorks, mesh it in Ansys, and define fiberglass plies in ACP.

02

Compare design options

Model axial compression, pure bending, and torsion for six- and eight-ply layups; examine deformation and continue checking stresses and failure criteria.

03

Check against testing

Verify the model and plan physical sample testing to compare measured behavior with the analysis.

Current stage: six- and eight-ply load studies
